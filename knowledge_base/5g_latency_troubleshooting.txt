Problem: High Latency
Symptoms: Latency exceeds operational limits.
Possible causes: Cell congestion, backhaul congestion, retransmissions, core delay, poor radio conditions.
Indicators: High PRB use, packet loss, retransmissions, queueing delay.
Diagnostic checks: Check PRB utilization, retransmission rate, packet loss, and backhaul utilization.
Recommended actions: Investigate overloaded cells and transport capacity.
