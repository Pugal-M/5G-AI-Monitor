Problem: QoS and Queueing Instability
Symptoms: Variable delay and jitter, especially for real-time traffic.
Possible causes: QoS policy mismatch, queue saturation, traffic prioritization failure.
Indicators: High queue depth, drops in priority queues, variable delay.
Diagnostic checks: Check QoS policy, queue depth, DSCP mapping, and scheduler queues.
Recommended actions: Correct QoS policy and reserve capacity for critical traffic.
