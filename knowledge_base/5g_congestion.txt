Problem: 5G Cell Congestion
Symptoms: High latency, low throughput, jitter, and scheduling delay during busy periods.
Possible causes: High PRB utilization, too many active users, overloaded cell resources.
Indicators: PRB utilization, active user count, queue depth, and scheduler delay increase together.
Diagnostic checks: Check PRB utilization, active users, and cell resource utilization.
Recommended actions: Load balance users, add capacity, or optimize scheduler resources.
