Problem: Radio Interference
Symptoms: High jitter, retransmissions, unstable throughput and degraded SINR.
Possible causes: Neighbor-cell interference, external RF interference, poor frequency planning.
Indicators: SINR deterioration, intermittent BLER and retransmission spikes.
Diagnostic checks: Check interference levels and neighboring-cell configuration.
Recommended actions: Correct interference source and optimize frequency planning.
