Problem: Low Throughput
Symptoms: Throughput is below expected service capacity.
Possible causes: Congestion, low signal quality, low CQI, backhaul limitation, interference.
Indicators: High PRB utilization, low CQI/SINR, retransmissions, transport bottleneck.
Diagnostic checks: Check PRB utilization, CQI, SINR, and backhaul capacity.
Recommended actions: Resolve congestion, radio quality, or transport limitation.
