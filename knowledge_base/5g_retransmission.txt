Problem: Packet Retransmissions
Symptoms: Latency and jitter increase while effective throughput falls.
Possible causes: Radio errors, poor link quality, packet loss, HARQ retransmissions.
Indicators: High retransmission rate, BLER, packet loss, low CQI.
Diagnostic checks: Check retransmission rate, BLER, packet loss, and radio quality.
Recommended actions: Repair radio quality and investigate loss sources.
