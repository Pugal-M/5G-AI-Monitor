Problem: High Jitter
Symptoms: Packet delay varies significantly, disrupting voice and real-time services.
Possible causes: Queueing, variable load, backhaul instability, retransmissions, routing changes.
Indicators: Variable queue depth, packet loss, transport delay variation.
Diagnostic checks: Check queue depth, packet loss, transport stability, and QoS policy.
Recommended actions: Stabilize queues and investigate transport changes.
