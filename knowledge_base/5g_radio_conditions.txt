Problem: Poor 5G Radio Conditions
Symptoms: Low throughput, retransmissions, high latency at the cell edge.
Possible causes: Low SINR, low CQI, weak RSRP/RSRQ, coverage gaps.
Indicators: Low CQI/SINR and high BLER.
Diagnostic checks: Check SINR, CQI, RSRP, RSRQ, and UE location.
Recommended actions: Optimize antenna parameters and resolve coverage problems.
