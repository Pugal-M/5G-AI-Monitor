Problem: Backhaul Congestion
Symptoms: High latency, degraded throughput, variable jitter, and transport packet loss.
Possible causes: Insufficient transport capacity, saturated backhaul link, routing delay.
Indicators: High backhaul utilization, increased transport latency, queueing, packet drops.
Diagnostic checks: Check backhaul utilization, transport latency, packet loss, and routing path.
Recommended actions: Increase transport capacity or investigate saturated links.
